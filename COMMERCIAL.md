## What is *not* commercial use

These are fine under the noncommercial licence, no permission needed:

- a person, class, hackerspace, or club building boards for their own use or for
  teaching;
- recovering the actual cost of materials (PCB fab, parts, shipping) when you
  build boards for others in a non-profit setting;
- a school, university, public research or public-safety organisation using the
  work, regardless of how that organisation is funded.

If you are not sure which side of the line you are on, ask first.
